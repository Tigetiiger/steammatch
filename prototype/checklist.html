Each screen carries a live component budget and a round-trip counter — every interaction with Discord is one network round trip and one re-render, which is the thing the three options really trade against each other.

The hard limits being respected: classic components allow 5 rows × 5 buttons and a select menu eats a whole row on its own; Components V2 allows 40 components counting nested ones, and a Container holds 10 children. 25 clickable checkmarks plus navigation does not fit in either system — that is the constraint that forces a choice below.
